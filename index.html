Frequently Asked Questions

How much does birthday decoration cost in Bengaluru?

Birthday decoration with Mela Celebrations typically starts from a base package price and scales with theme complexity, balloon volume, and add-ons like backdrops or floral work. Request a custom quote through our Customizer for an exact price based on your event size and theme.

How far in advance should I book event decoration?

We recommend booking at least 5–7 days in advance for standard packages, and 2–3 weeks in advance for weddings or large corporate events to secure your preferred date and theme materials.

Yes. We provide balloon and theme décor for corporate events including product launches, office celebrations, and brand activations across Bengaluru, with options to match brand colors and logos.

Does Mela Celebrations handle setup and takedown?
